Smart Alarm Calendar is an open source Android application designed for shift workers, flexible schedules, and non-weekly routines.

Key Features:

- Interactive Calendar: Assign alarm presets to specific dates or multi-date selections with a single tap.
- Customizable Presets: Create preset templates with multiple alarms (e.g. Morning Shift, Afternoon Shift, Day Off).
- Home Screen Widget: View today's and tomorrow's alarms in a clean Proton-style layout with direct toggle switches.
- Full-Screen Lock Screen Alarm: Swipe-up gesture to dismiss alarms reliably without accidental touches.
- Pre-Alarm Notification: Silent notification 1 hour prior with a quick-action "Dismiss" button.
- Customization: Dark Theme, Light Theme, custom alarm background colors, ringtone picker, and snooze options.

Completely free, open source, ad-free, and privacy-respecting without tracking or proprietary SDKs.
